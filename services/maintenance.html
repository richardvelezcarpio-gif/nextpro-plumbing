ONGOING BUILDING MAINTENANCE

One maintenance partner. One point of contact.

Instead of finding a different contractor for every issue, work with NextPro as your ongoing maintenance partner for routine care, inspections and repair needs.

When a covered issue is reported, we evaluate the problem, coordinate the necessary work and help restore the affected system or area as efficiently as possible.

Your agreement defines what is included. Services, frequency and response conditions are tailored to the building; work outside the agreed scope is reviewed separately.

PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

Fix small problems before they become expensive problems.

Periodic inspections look for visible leaks, wear and deterioration so that repairs can be prioritized early. These examples show how an unattended issue can develop—not a prediction for every property.

Small leak→Water damage
Loose fixture→Larger repair
Slow drain→Possible blockage
Damaged caulking→Moisture problems
Door alignment issue→Damaged hardware or frame
